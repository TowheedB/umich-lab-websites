SA
Principal Investigator

Sara Aton

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Sara Aton received her PhD in Neuroscience from Washington University in St. Louis, where she worked with Erik Herzog, and completed postdoctoral training with Marcos Frank at the University of Pennsylvania. She joined the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan as an Assistant Professor in 2012, was promoted to Associate Professor with tenure in 2019, and to Professor in 2024. Her research examines how sensory experience and sleep shape neural circuits, with a focus on the mechanisms underlying plasticity and memory formation.
