Principal Investigator

Monica Dus

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology; Assistant Vice President for Research (Natural Sciences and Engineering) and Director of the Office of National Laboratories

Monica Dus earned her Ph.D. in Molecular Biology from the Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory School of Biological Sciences in 2008. She is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan and serves as Assistant Vice President for Research and inaugural Director of U-M's Office of National Laboratories. Her Laboratory of Molecular Nutrition studies the interplay between food, genes, and physiology, with a focus on nutrigenomics, nutrient sensing, and the effects of diet on the brain. She is a 2023 Guggenheim Fellow, a 2023-2024 White House Fellow, and a recipient of the NSF CAREER Award and NIH New Innovator Award.
