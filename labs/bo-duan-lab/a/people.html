Bo Duan

Associate Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Bo Duan is an Associate Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan, where he founded his laboratory in 2016. His lab investigates the development, organization, and function of neural circuits underlying somatosensory modalities such as pain, itch, touch, and temperature sensation. The group uses mouse genetics, histochemistry, neuroanatomical tracing, electrophysiology, in vivo imaging, and behavioral analyses.
