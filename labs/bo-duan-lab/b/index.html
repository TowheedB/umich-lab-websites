Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology · University of Michigan

Mapping the neural circuits that allow the nervous system to sense pain, itch, and touch.

The Bo Duan lab studies how the nervous system perceives and processes somatosensory information, including temperature, touch, pain, and itch.

Research themes

All research →
01

Thermal vs. mechanical pain transmission

Identifying the spinal neurons responsible for transmitting different types of pain.

02

Mechanical itch circuits

Identifying the spinal circuits and dedicated hair afferents that transmit mechanical itch signals.

03

Peripheral inputs for pain and itch

Characterizing the peripheral sensory inputs that drive painful and itchy sensations, including dedicated skin-to-brain circuits for temperature sensation.

04

Descending modulation of pain and itch

Dissecting the descending neural pathways that regulate pain and itch responses.
