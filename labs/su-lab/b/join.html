Work with us

We are looking for curious, collaborative people who want to help answer the questions that drive the lab.

Openings

The lab is recruiting postdoctoral researchers from a broad variety of backgrounds (neuroscience, physiology, immunology, body-brain interactions); apply by emailing a cover letter describing research interests and a CV with three references. Ph.D., M.D., and Master's students interested in the lab's interdisciplinary work should email to discuss rotation projects. There are currently no undergraduate openings, but applicants are encouraged to check back. Exchange scholars and summer researchers may reach out with a CV and summary of experience. The lab emphasizes mentorship tailored to individual career goals in a collaborative and supportive environment.

Graduate students

Apply through MCDB or PIBS and mention your interest in the lab.

Postdocs

Send a CV, a brief statement of interests, and contact details for references.

Undergraduates

Email with your year, relevant coursework, and the kind of project you hope to join.
