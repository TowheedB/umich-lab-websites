Su Lab · University of Michigan

Mapping the neural circuits that allow the brain to sense the lungs and regulate physiology.

The Su Lab (Body-Brain Neural Circuits Lab) studies body-brain communication in health and disease, using the lung-brain axis as a model for interoception.
