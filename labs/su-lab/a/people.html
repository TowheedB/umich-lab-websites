Who we are

The Su Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

YS
Principal Investigator

Yujuan Su

Assistant Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Yujuan Su holds a Ph.D. in neuroscience from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and completed postdoctoral training at UC San Diego, where she studied lung-innervating neurons and brainstem control of airway responses. She has received the Parker B. Francis Fellowship and an American Heart Association Career Development Award. She launched the Body-Brain Neural Circuits Lab in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan in July 2026.
