Principal Investigator

Ariangela J. Kozik

Assistant Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Ariangela J. Kozik earned a B.S. in Biotechnology from Calvin College and a Ph.D. in Comparative Pathobiology from Purdue University, followed by training in Personalized and Genomic Medicine at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus. She was an NIH-fellowship-supported postdoctoral fellow studying the respiratory microbiome and asthma and then a Research Investigator in pulmonary medicine at Michigan Medicine, focusing on microbial-immune relationships in adult asthma with an emphasis on precision approaches. She is now an Assistant Professor in MCDB with affiliations in Internal Medicine (Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine), the Center for Computational Medicine and Bioinformatics, and AI and Digital Health Innovation. She co-founded the Black Microbiologists Association in 2020.
