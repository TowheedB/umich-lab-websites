Principal Investigator

Amy Chang

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Amy Chang received an A.B. in Biochemistry from Harvard University and a Ph.D. in Cell Biology from Yale University, followed by postdoctoral training at Yale and at the Whitehead Institute at MIT. She is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. Her lab uses budding yeast to study how cells respond to stress, in particular how misfolded proteins in the endoplasmic reticulum trigger signaling, transcription, and mitochondrial remodeling that allow cells to survive.
