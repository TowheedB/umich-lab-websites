Who we are

The Wierzbicki Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

AW
Principal Investigator

Andrzej Wierzbicki

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Andrzej Wierzbicki received his Ph.D. from the University of Warsaw, Poland, and was a postdoctoral research associate at Washington University in St. Louis before joining the University of Michigan faculty in 2009. His research asks how DNA is packaged and how that packaging relates to gene activity in the nucleus and in plastids, using plant experimental systems. He teaches genetics (BIO 305) and epigenetics (MCDB 462).
