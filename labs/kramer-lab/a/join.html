We are looking for curious, collaborative people who want to help answer the questions that drive the lab.

Openings

The lab welcomes graduate applicants from the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology program (PhD or Master's) and the Neuroscience Graduate Program (PhD only). Candidates for postdoctoral and technical positions should reach out directly to Paul Kramer to inquire about available opportunities. Dr. Kramer emphasizes giving trainees foundational knowledge and tools to pursue shared research interests, and believes anyone driven by intrinsic motivation can become a scientist.

Graduate students

Apply through MCDB or PIBS and mention your interest in the lab.

Postdocs

Send a CV, a brief statement of interests, and contact details for references.

Undergraduates

Email with your year, relevant coursework, and the kind of project you hope to join.
