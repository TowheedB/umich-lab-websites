Who we are

The Kramer Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

PF
Principal Investigator

Paul F. Kramer

Assistant Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Paul Kramer earned a B.A. in Psychology with a Neuroscience concentration from Grinnell College (2009) and a Ph.D. in Neuroscience from Oregon Health and Science University (2016), where he worked with John T. Williams. He completed postdoctoral training at the NIH with Zayd Khaliq studying dopaminergic axon physiology, including synaptic-like axo-axonal transmission from striatal cholinergic interneurons onto dopamine fibers. He opened his lab at the University of Michigan in August 2023 and received the Brain and Behavior Research Foundation Young Investigator Award in 2025 for nicotine research.
