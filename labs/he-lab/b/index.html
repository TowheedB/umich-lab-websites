He Lab · University of Michigan

Discovering how plants recognize pathogens and activate precisely controlled immune responses.

The He lab is part of the Molecular Plant-Microbe Interaction (MPMI) laboratory at the University of Michigan, a shared research group co-led by Ping He and Libo Shan.

Research themes

The questions that organize our work.

1

Activation and attenuation of plant PRR complexes and signaling

Examines how plants activate and regulate pattern recognition receptor signaling pathways in response to microbial threats.

 2

Transcriptional, post-transcriptional, and post-translational regulation of plant immunity

Investigates multiple levels of gene expression control, including alternative splicing and phosphorylation, that govern plant defense again…

 3

Coordination of plant immunity, growth, and development

Studies how plants balance immune responses with growth and developmental processes under environmental stress.

 4

Regulation of plant autoimmunity

Explores mechanisms, including E3 ligases, chaperones, and calcium signaling, that prevent plants from mounting excessive self-damaging immu…

 5

Immunomodulatory phytocytokines in plant immunity

Analyzes peptide signaling molecules that coordinate and fine-tune plant immune activation.

 6

Cotton disease resistance and abiotic stress adaptation

Applies functional and translational genomics to identify disease-resistant traits and improve stress resilience in cotton.
