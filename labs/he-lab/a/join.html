Openings

The lab is seeking postdoctoral fellows, PhD students, and undergraduate researchers. Postdoctoral applicants should send a cover letter, CV with publication list, statement of research interests, and contact information for three references to Ping He or Libo Shan.

Graduate students

Apply through MCDB or PIBS and mention your interest in the lab.

Postdocs

Send a CV, a brief statement of interests, and contact details for references.

Undergraduates

Email with your year, relevant coursework, and the kind of project you hope to join.
