Principal Investigator

Ping He

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Ping He is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. He received his B.S. from China Agricultural University, his M.S. from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and his Ph.D. from Kansas State University, followed by postdoctoral training at Harvard Medical School. His research focuses on the activation and signaling mechanisms of host immunity upon pathogen infection, using Arabidopsis as a model, and on how signaling crosstalk orchestrates plant responses to extrinsic and intrinsic signals, with applications toward improving crop resilience. He co-leads the Molecular Plant-Microbe Interaction laboratory with Professor Libo Shan.
