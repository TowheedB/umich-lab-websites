Who we are

The Buttitta Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

LB
Principal Investigator

Laura Buttitta

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Laura Buttitta earned her Ph.D. from Johns Hopkins University in 2004 and completed postdoctoral training with Bruce Edgar at the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center. She opened her lab in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan in January 2011. Her research focuses on how the cell cycle is shut off in quiescent cells, such as differentiated or stem cells, using Drosophila and mammalian systems. She teaches MCDB 440, Cell Cycle Control and Cancer.
