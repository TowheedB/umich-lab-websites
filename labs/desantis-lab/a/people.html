Principal Investigator

Morgan DeSantis

Assistant Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Morgan DeSantis is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. She earned her Ph.D. from the University of Pennsylvania. Her lab investigates how microtubule-associated motor proteins, especially cytoplasmic dynein, transport cellular cargo, how motor activity is regulated, and how motor dysfunction relates to neurodevelopmental disease, using single-molecule TIRF microscopy, live-cell fluorescence microscopy, protein biochemistry, and cryo-electron microscopy.
