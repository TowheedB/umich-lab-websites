Who we are

The Akaaboune Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

MA
Principal Investigator

Mohammed Akaaboune

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Mohammed Akaaboune earned his DEA and Ph.D. from the University of Pierre and Marie Curie (Paris 6) in France, and completed postdoctoral training in Jeff Lichtman's laboratory at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis, supported by a Human Frontier Science Program fellowship. He is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. His lab studies the regulation and dynamics of synaptic proteins at healthy and compromised neuromuscular junctions using optical imaging, molecular, cellular, and biochemical approaches in mice.
