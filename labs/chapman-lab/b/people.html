Who we are

MC
Principal Investigator

Matthew Chapman

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Matthew Chapman earned his Ph.D. in Microbiology from Indiana University in 1999 and was a Keck and NIH postdoctoral fellow in Scott Hultgren's laboratory at Washington University in St. Louis from 1999 to 2003. He joined the faculty of the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan in September 2003. His lab studies the structure, function, and biogenesis of bacterial amyloid fibers, using E. coli curli as a model for understanding amyloid formation and its role in biofilms and disease.
