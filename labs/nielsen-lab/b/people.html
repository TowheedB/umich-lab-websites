Principal Investigator

Erik Nielsen

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Erik Nielsen earned his Ph.D. in 1997 from the Plant Research Lab at Michigan State University and was a postdoctoral fellow at EMBL Heidelberg, Germany, from 1998 to 2001. He is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. His research examines polarized secretion during cellular differentiation, focusing on the membrane trafficking pathways that deliver cell wall components in plants and how this relates to plant growth and development.
