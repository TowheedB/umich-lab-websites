Who we are

The Jakob Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

UJ
Principal Investigator

Ursula Jakob

Patricia S. Yaeger Collegiate Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Ursula Jakob received her B.S. (1991) and Ph.D. (1995) from Regensburg University, Germany, and held postdoctoral and research scientist positions at the University of Michigan from 1996 to 2001 before joining the faculty. She is the Patricia S. Yaeger Collegiate Professor in Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology, a University of Michigan Biological Scholar, a recipient of the Burroughs Wellcome Fund Career Award in Biomedical Sciences (2000), and was elected to the Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities in 2014. Her NIH-funded research examines the role of oxidative stress in host defense and aging, redox-regulated chaperones, and the functions of inorganic polyphosphate.
