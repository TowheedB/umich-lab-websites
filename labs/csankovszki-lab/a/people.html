Who we are

The Csankovszki Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

GC
Principal Investigator

Gyorgyi Csankovszki

Arthur F. Thurnau Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology; Associate Chair for Graduate Studies

Gyorgyi Csankovszki earned her Ph.D. from MIT in 2001 and completed postdoctoral training at UC Berkeley from 2001 to 2005 before joining the University of Michigan. She is a Professor and Arthur F. Thurnau Professor in Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology, where she also serves as Associate Chair for Graduate Studies. Her lab uses C. elegans dosage compensation as a model to study how higher-order chromosome structure and organization affect gene expression, combining biochemical, genetic, and microscopic imaging approaches.
