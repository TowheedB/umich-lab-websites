We are looking for curious, collaborative people who want to help answer the questions that drive the lab.

Openings

The lab is actively recruiting at multiple levels. Postdoctoral applicants should send a CV with publication list and a cover letter explaining their research interests. Graduate students from the MCDB, PIBS, and Neuroscience graduate programs are welcome and should reach out to discuss setting up a rotation. Prospective undergraduates should email a description of their research interests and goals along with an unofficial transcript.

Graduate students

Apply through MCDB or PIBS and mention your interest in the lab.

Postdocs

Send a CV, a brief statement of interests, and contact details for references.

Undergraduates

Email with your year, relevant coursework, and the kind of project you hope to join.
