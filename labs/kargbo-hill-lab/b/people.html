Who we are

The Kargbo-Hill Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

SE
Principal Investigator

Sarah E. Kargbo-Hill

Assistant Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Sarah E. Kargbo-Hill earned a B.S. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Penn State University (2012), where she worked with Melissa Rolls on microtubule polarity in Drosophila neurons, and a Ph.D. in Cell Biology from Yale University (2018) with Daniel Colon-Ramos studying presynaptic development and autophagy in C. elegans. She completed postdoctoral training with Michael Ward at the NIH, using human iPSC-derived neurons to study neurodegenerative disease proteins such as TDP-43. She joined the MCDB department at the University of Michigan as an Assistant Professor in January 2024. Her training was supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, a BrightFocus Foundation Fellowship, and a K99 NIH/NIA Pathway to Independence Award.
