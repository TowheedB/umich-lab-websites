Schiefelbein Lab · University of Michigan

Using plant development to uncover the molecular rules that give cells distinct identities.

The Schiefelbein Lab seeks to understand the molecular mechanisms that enable distinct cell fates to arise during the development of multicellular organisms.

What we study Meet the team
  • Cell differentiation
  • pattern formation
  • Arabidopsis root epidermis
