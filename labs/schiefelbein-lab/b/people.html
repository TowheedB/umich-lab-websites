Who we are

The Schiefelbein Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

JS
Principal Investigator

John Schiefelbein

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

John Schiefelbein received his Ph.D. from the University of Wisconsin at Madison in 1987 and completed postdoctoral work at Michigan State University (1987-1989). He received a National Science Foundation Young Investigator Award in 1992. His laboratory studies root epidermis development in the flowering plant Arabidopsis thaliana as a model for understanding how distinct cell fates arise during the development of multicellular organisms.
