Principal Investigator

Ann L. Miller

Arthur F. Thurnau Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Ann L. Miller earned a B.A. in Biochemistry from Gustavus Adolphus College and a Ph.D. in Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry from Yale University in Tony Koleske's lab, then completed postdoctoral training in cell biology with Bill Bement at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. She joined the University of Michigan MCDB department as an Assistant Professor in 2011, was promoted to Associate Professor in 2018 and Professor in 2024, and was named an Arthur F. Thurnau Professor in 2025. She chaired the Signaling by Adhesion Receptors Gordon Research Conference in 2022 and has received the Class of 1923 Memorial Teaching Award (2018) and an Exceptional Mentor Award (2017). She also serves as MCDB's Community Engagement Faculty Liaison.
