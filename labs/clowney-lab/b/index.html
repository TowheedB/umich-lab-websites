Clowney Lab · University of Michigan

Discovering how development builds neural circuits that transform sensory information into behavior.

The Clowney Lab investigates how neural circuits that drive survival behaviors (feeding, fighting, fleeing, mating, parenting, and sleeping) are organized in the brain and how that organization is produced during development.
