EJ
Principal Investigator

E. Josie Clowney

Associate Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

E. Josie Clowney is a University of Michigan alumna from Grand Rapids, Michigan. She earned her PhD studying olfactory receptor choice with Stavros Lomvardas at UCSF and completed postdoctoral work on neural circuits for mate choice with Vanessa Ruta at Rockefeller University. She founded her lab in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan in 2017 and was promoted to Associate Professor with tenure in 2024. Her awards include fellowships from the Sloan, Pew, Rita Allen, and McKnight Foundations.
