Who we are

The Vecchiarelli Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

AV
Principal Investigator

Anthony Vecchiarelli

Associate Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology and Associate Professor of Biophysics

Anthony Vecchiarelli earned his HBSc and PhD in Molecular Genetics and Microbiology at the University of Toronto with Barbara Funnell. He completed postdoctoral training at the National Institutes of Health with Kiyoshi Mizuuchi as a Nancy Nossal Postdoctoral Fellow. At the University of Michigan his laboratory uses genetics, cell biology, biochemistry, and bioinformatics to understand how bacteria spatially organize fundamental processes in the cell.
