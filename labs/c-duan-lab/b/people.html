Principal Investigator

Cunming Duan

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Cunming Duan is a Professor in the Department of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan. He earned his doctoral degree from the University of Tokyo and completed postdoctoral fellowships at the University of Washington and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. His lab studies the regulation, function, and evolution of nutrient-sensitive insulin and insulin-like growth factor (IIS) signaling and how it controls cellular plasticity under stresses such as hypoxia, nutrient deficiency, and acidosis, with recent work extending to calcium metabolism, bone mineralization, renal mineral balance, and hypoxia biology.
