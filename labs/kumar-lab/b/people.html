Principal Investigator

Anuj Kumar

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Anuj Kumar earned his Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences from Wright State University and completed postdoctoral training at Yale University in Michael Snyder's laboratory, where he held American Cancer Society Postdoctoral and Leslie Warner fellowships. At the University of Michigan he has been named a Biological Scholar, a March of Dimes Basil O'Connor Scholar, and an American Cancer Society Research Scholar, and he serves on the Faculty of 1000 (Genomics section). His lab uses functional genomics, proteomics, genetics, cell biology, and biochemistry in budding yeast and Candida albicans to study signaling pathways underlying eukaryotic cell growth, particularly the pseudohyphal growth transition.
