Who we are

The Simmons Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

LS
Principal Investigator

Lyle Simmons

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Lyle Simmons earned a B.S. from Alma College and a Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Michigan State University. He was a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biology at MIT before joining the University of Michigan in 2008. His laboratory studies how bacteria duplicate and repair their DNA, using Bacillus subtilis as a model organism.
