Who we are

The Wittkopp Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Ecology and Evolutionary Biology; Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

PW
Principal Investigator

Patricia Wittkopp

Deborah E. Goldberg Distinguished University Professor of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology and Arthur F. Thurnau Professor; Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Patricia (Trisha) Wittkopp earned a B.S. from the University of Michigan (1997) and a Ph.D. from the University of Wisconsin-Madison (2002), then was a Damon Runyon Fellow with Andy Clark at Cornell (2002-2005) before founding her lab at Michigan in August 2005. She served as EEB Department Chair (2020-2023), LSA Associate Dean for Natural Sciences (2023-2025), and is LSA Associate Dean for Research and Graduate Education (2025-present). Her honors include Guggenheim Fellow, AAAS Fellow, Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellow, a Humboldt Research Award, and the Margaret Dayhoff Mid-Career Award. Her lab studies the genetic basis of phenotypic evolution, with an emphasis on gene regulation in Drosophila and yeast.
