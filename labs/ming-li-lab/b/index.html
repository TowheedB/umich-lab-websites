Ming Li Lab · University of Michigan

Understanding how lysosomes maintain cellular quality control and how these systems fail in disease.

The Ming Li Lab investigates the lysosome: how it is born (biogenesis), how it is regulated to stay functional (quality control), and how it communicates with other organelles.
