Who we are

The Ming Li Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

ML
Principal Investigator

Ming Li

Associate Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology; Associate Chair for Research and Facilities

Ming Li grew up in Jingdezhen, China, and was the first in his family to attend college. He earned a B.S. from Fudan University in Shanghai and a Ph.D. from the University of Massachusetts-Amherst, followed by postdoctoral training at UMass-Amherst and Cornell University. He is an Associate Professor in MCDB at the University of Michigan, where he also serves as Associate Chair for Research and Facilities. His lab studies lysosomal membrane protein quality control and lysosome biogenesis in the context of neurodegeneration, cancer, and lysosomal storage diseases; he received an NIH R35 award in 2024.
