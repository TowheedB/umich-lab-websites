Who we are

The Stockbridge Lab brings together researchers across career stages in the Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology at the University of Michigan.

RS
Principal Investigator

Randy Stockbridge

Professor of Molecular, Cellular, and Developmental Biology

Randy Stockbridge earned an A.B. in Molecular Biology from Princeton University (2005) and a Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Biophysics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (2010). She established her laboratory at the University of Michigan in 2016. In 2024-2025 she completed a sabbatical in the Paulino lab studying cryo-EM with support from the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation. Her lab studies membrane transport proteins, the channels and energy-coupled pumps that act as molecular gatekeepers of the cell.
